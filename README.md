Sun Son Solar

Team Name: DOMS SUNSOLAR

Project Description

Sun Son Solar is a web-based platform for a solar energy company that provides information about its solar services, including consultation, installation, and maintenance. The homepage features call-to-action buttons for requesting a free quotation and scheduling a service, making it easier for customers to inquire about solar solutions.

The project promotes the benefits of solar energy, such as cost savings, environmental sustainability, and energy independence. Developed using CodeIgniter 4 and the Model-View-Controller (MVC) pattern, the website includes a homepage, customer registration, login, logout, and database integration.
